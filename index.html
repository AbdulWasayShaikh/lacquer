FAQ

Before you book.

How long will you keep my car?

A wash is same day. A one-step correction is one day. Ceramic Armour is two days, because the coating needs time to cure before the car goes back on the road. Full body PPF takes three to four days.

Will polishing thin out my paint?

Every correction removes a few microns of clear coat, which is why we measure first. If a panel reads thin, we use a lighter pad and polish there, or we tell you it should be left alone.

Ceramic or PPF — which do I need?

Ceramic gives gloss and makes the car easy to clean, but it will not stop a stone chip. PPF stops chips and light scratches. Many owners put PPF on the front and ceramic over the whole car.

Can you fix deep scratches?

If your fingernail catches in it, the scratch has gone through the clear coat and polishing cannot remove it. We can make it much less visible, and we will tell you honestly before we start.

Do you pick up the car?

Yes, inside DHA, Clifton and PECHS for corrections, ceramic and PPF jobs. Ask when you book.
